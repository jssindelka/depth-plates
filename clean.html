[ 02 — OPERATING PROCEDURE ]

THE SAME THREE-PHASE SEQUENCE RUNS FOR EVERY PLATE. SCROLL DRIVES THE SEQUENCE; FREE INSPECTION HANDS THE CAMERA TO THE OPERATOR.

A

FLAT PLATE

THE IMAGE SITS DOCKED IN THE GRID, UNPROCESSED AND AT FULL RESOLUTION. AS SCROLL BEGINS THE FRAME WIPES ACROSS THE DATA COLUMN UNTIL IT FILLS THE STAGE.

B

DEPTH EXTRUSION

THE IMAGE IS MAPPED ONTO A FINE MESH AND EVERY VERTEX IS DISPLACED ALONG Z BY ITS DEPTH VALUE. THE SURFACE TILTS; THE PICTURE ITSELF IS NOT ALTERED.

C

DOLLY / FREE ZOOM

THE CAMERA TRAVELS INTO THE VOLUME TOWARD THE PLATE'S FOCUS COORDINATE. PRESS ENTER SPACE TO TAKE CONTROL: ORBIT, PAN AND ZOOM UP TO ×16.
