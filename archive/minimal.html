Method

Three quiet steps between a picture and a place.

Nothing is modelled by hand. Every point you see is a pixel of the original photograph, moved to the distance it was photographed at.

The still

The plate sits in the page as the untouched photograph, in its original colour. As you begin to scroll, its frame widens until the image fills the view.

The relief

A depth map pushes each pixel forward or back. Distant areas soften toward the paper, the way haze works outdoors.

The approach

The camera travels toward a point you choose per plate. Step inside to take over: orbit, pan, and zoom up to sixteen times.

Questions

Before you add your own.

Which photographs work best?

Landscape-format images with a clear foreground, subject and background. The more distinct the distances in the scene, the more there is to move through. Flat subjects such as documents or patterns have nothing to separate.

Do I need to prepare a depth map?

No. If a plate has no depth map, one is estimated in the browser when the page loads. For a published site, save that map once with the button inside the viewer and reference it in the configuration, so visitors do not wait for the model.

Does my image get uploaded anywhere?

No. The depth model is downloaded once, about 25 to 50 MB, and then runs on your own device. The photograph itself stays on your computer.

Why does the page need a local server?

Browsers refuse to hand image files opened directly from disk to the graphics card. Any static server works; the simplest is python3 -m http.server inside the project folder.
